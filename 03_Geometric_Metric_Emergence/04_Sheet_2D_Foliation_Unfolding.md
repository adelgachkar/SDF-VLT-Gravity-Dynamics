# Sheet 2D Foliation Unfolding

## 1. The Unit-Base Cuboid Geometry
By the unit-base cuboid geometry ($A=1$):
- 2 faces (floor and ceiling) act as the boundary sheets
- 4 lateral faces are responsible for height accumulation and the creation of the third dimension ($h$)

The expansion of space arises from the sequential layering of these sheets under surface tensile stress.
