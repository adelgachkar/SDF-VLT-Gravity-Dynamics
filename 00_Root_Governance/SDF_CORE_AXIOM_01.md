# SDF Core Axiom 01 (The Non-Constraint Foundation)

## 1. First Axiom — The Non-Constraint Foundation
The ontological ground carries no a priori constraint, no rigid geometry, and no pre-imposed metric. All physical attributes — space, time, matter, and inertia — take shape through **Delimitation**.

## 2. Emergence of the Boundary and Primary Stress
With the first act of delimitation ($\mathcal{C}_{\text{id}}$), the primary discontinuity takes form, and tensile stress $\sigma_{\text{wall}}$ appears at the boundary.
