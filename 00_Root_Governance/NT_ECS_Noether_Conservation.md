# Variational Principle and Boundary Matching (Israel Junction Conditions)

## 1. Total Action
Within the Structural Delineation Framework (SDF), the total spacetime action comprises the Hilbert–Einstein term, the boundary field, the Gibbons–Hawking–York (GHY) boundary term, and the wall surface-energy term:

$$S = S_{\text{EH}} + S_{\Phi} + S_{\text{GHY}} + S_{\Sigma}$$

where the surface action of the boundary wall $\Sigma$ is given explicitly by:

$$S_{\Sigma} = -\int_{\Sigma} d^3\xi \sqrt{-q} \, \sigma_{\text{wall}}(\Phi)$$

Here $q = \det(q_{ab})$ is the determinant of the induced metric $q_{ab}$ on the 3D hypersurface of the boundary $\Sigma$, and $\sigma_{\text{wall}}(\Phi)$ denotes the wall surface tension generated by the field gradient.

## 2. Boundary Matching Conditions (Israel Junction Conditions)
Varying the action with respect to the metric in the presence of the surface distribution of the wall, the jump in the extrinsic curvature $[K_{ab}] \equiv K_{ab}^+ - K_{ab}^-$ is balanced by the surface stress tensor $S_{ab}$:

$$[K_{ab}] - q_{ab}[K] = -8\pi G \, S_{ab}$$

Assuming surface isotropy for the wall ($S_{ab} = -\sigma_{\text{wall}} q_{ab}$):

$$[K_{ab}] - q_{ab}[K] = 8\pi G \, \sigma_{\text{wall}} \, q_{ab}$$

Taking the trace of the above relation by contracting with $q^{ab}$ (noting that in 3 spacelike dimensions on the boundary $q^{ab}q_{ab} = 3$):

$$[K] - 3[K] = 8\pi G \, \sigma_{\text{wall}} (3) \implies -2[K] = 24\pi G \, \sigma_{\text{wall}}$$

Therefore the jump in the trace of the extrinsic curvature is obtained exactly as:

$$[K] = -12\pi G \, \sigma_{\text{wall}}$$

This relation (the modified Israel junction condition) provides the **geometric closure** and sign invariance between the two distinct spacetime regions (VOID and Lattice Wall).

### Extended Barrabès–Israel Null Limit
In the transition to null (lightlike) void boundaries ($ds^2 = 0$) with the transverse vector $\ell^\mu$ ($\ell_\mu \ell^\mu = 0$ and $\ell^a q_{ab} = 0$):

$$[\mathcal{K}_{ab}] = -8\pi G \, \sigma_{\text{wall}} \, \ell_a \ell_b$$

Due to the Lorentzian nature of the time dimension and the temporal-gauge orientation, the phase sign flip in the wave geometry is co-aligned, and differential conservation across the boundary is guaranteed.

## 3. Distributional Conservation Proof
The total stress–energy tensor contains both bulk components and distributional surface effects:

$$T_{\text{total}}^{\mu\nu} = T_{(\Phi)}^{\mu\nu} \Theta(\mathcal{M}) + S^{ab} e^\mu_a e^\nu_b \, \delta(\Sigma)$$

Using the Bianchi identity and the symmetry condition $\nabla_a S^{ab} = 0$:

$$\nabla_\mu T_{\text{total}}^{\mu\nu} = 0 \iff [T_{\mu\nu}^{(\Phi)} n^\mu n^\nu] + S^{ab} \bar{K}_{ab} = 0$$

where $n^\mu$ is the unit normal to the hypersurface $\Sigma$ and $\bar{K}_{ab} = \frac{1}{2}(K_{ab}^+ + K_{ab}^-)$ is the average of the extrinsic curvature on the two sides of the boundary. This identity guarantees that "wall stress" is not an external assumption but the necessary consequence of energy–momentum divergence conservation in a non-flat structure.
