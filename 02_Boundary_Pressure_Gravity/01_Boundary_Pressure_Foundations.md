# Boundary Pressure Foundations

## 1. Axiomatic Overview
The boundary pressure formalism defines emergent gravitational behavior not as an inherent fundamental attractive force, but as an effective boundary stress generated by the topological impedance of the underlying discrete lattice $\mathcal{L}$.

## 2. Formal Delineation
Given the generative void-lattice coupling, boundary stress $\Pi_{\partial}$ arises as:
$$\Pi_{\partial} = -\nabla_{\mathbf{n}} \mathcal{E}_{\text{lattice}} + \mathcal{T}_{\text{topological}}$$

Where:
- $\nabla_{\mathbf{n}}$ is the normal gradient across the boundary interface.
- $\mathcal{E}_{\text{lattice}}$ represents the ground-state lattice energy density.
- $\mathcal{T}_{\text{topological}}$ enforces the local conservation constraints derived from [[01_Canonical_Chain_Engine/02_Cid_Delimitation_Anchors]].
